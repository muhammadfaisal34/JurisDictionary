My name is Dr. Frederick David Graves. The lovely lady in the photo with me is my wife Kathryn. Together we are promoting Truth, Justice, and the American Way by making "How to Win in Court" easy for people like you to learn and share.

Step-by-step. Quick & easy!

I was 39 when I started law school. Before that I worked as a carpenter, fisherman, boat builder, and odd jobs. If I had legal problems, I was like 4 out of 5 of you—I could not afford to hire a lawyer.

So, I became one in 1986, passing the bar and very soon discovering how truly easy it is to use the rules of court to win cases for my clients.

In 1997 I started this online course to help everyone who can't afford a lawyer by giving affordable legal know-how and solid confidence to tens of thousands of people for the past 29 years ... good people who are winning without a lawyer!
