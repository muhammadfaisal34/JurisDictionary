Objections Control Judges !

Tactics

TV lawyers aren't real lawyers.

They say, "Objection!" without stating any grounds.

"Objection" is a good thing to say ...

• IF you know what it means

• IF you know why you should say it

• IF you know when you should say it

• And IF you know what grounds to state when you say it

TV lawyers just blurt out "Objection". That gets you nothing in a real court.

Objections without grounds are useless.

Objections without grounds don't control judges.

Objections without grounds cannot do what they are designed to do.

Objections without grounds fall short of their powerful case-winning purpose.

Learn how to object properly.

This class makes it easy.

Prove the facts with discovery.

Move the court for judgment.
