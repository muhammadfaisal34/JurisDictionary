The "Game" of Court

Court is a simple back-and-forth game of words. It starts with the first filed papers. It lasts until the final verdict. Back-and-forth.

Once the first papers are filed, a battle of paper motions is fought to settle the case without going further. This is called the "flurry of motions".

If the case doesn't settle, the battle for evidence begins. In a civil case you have five (5) paper tools to get evidence: Requests for Admissions, Requests for Production, Interrogatories, Depositions, Subpoenas and other court orders.

Step-by-step you will get the evidence you need. Most lawyers never figure this out. You will understand every step!

Making the Record

Get everything in writing and admitted to the court record. AI cannot do this for you. It is essential to winning.

Here are just a few things you will learn - things AI does not understand:

• Object every time the judge makes a mistake.

• Object every time the judge allows your opponent to break the rules.

• Move the court with verbal and written motions.

• Cite controlling law in written memoranda.

• Set hearings for all motions that you want to argue at hearings.

• Set hearings for all of your opponent's motions that you want to argue.

• Do not set hearings for your opponent's motions, unless you want to argue them.

• Arrange for all hearings (and trials) to be recorded.

• Stick to "essential facts". Do not wander on things that don't matter.

You will learn all this and much more. You will learn it all very quickly. You'll be amazed how easy it really is!
