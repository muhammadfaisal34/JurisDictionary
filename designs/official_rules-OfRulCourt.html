Official Rules

Know the Rules that Control the Judge!

Use Google® to Find the Official Rules

First step is to know what rules you need to find.

Most states follow the same idea.

There are in most states (and in the federal realm):

  • * Rules of Civil Procedure
  • * Rules of Evidence
  • * Rules of Judicial Administration
  • * Rules of Judicial Conduct
  • * Rules of Criminal Procedure
  • * Rules of Appellate Procedure
  • * Etc.

If you wish to find the official rules of evidence for cases in the federal courts, Google® "Federal Rules of Evidence".

If you wish to find the official rules of evidence for cases in Ohio, Google® "Ohio Rules of Evidence".

Google® will give you multiple links to websites that discuss or present the rules.

But beware!

Pay careful attention to what Google® shows you.

Some of the links Google® shows you are not official legal sources!

If a link takes you to the website of a law office, you should know that what may be presented is not official. The website might provide valuable information you can use. You might want to stay and visit to see what the lawyers have to say. However, if the information you find is not issued by an official government website, you dare not trust it!

Only official rules convince judges to enter orders.

Most states provide a website put up by the state supreme court or the legislature. You should be able to rely on the official rules you find there.

Some states provide a website put up by the state bar association. You might be able to rely on the rules there, but bar's version is not official!

Always rely only on rules promulgated by government resources.

Rules presented by any other entity or individual are not official.

If you are looking for federal rules, be sure the website links Google® presents lead you to an authorized branch of the federal government ... not some law school, university, private corporation, law firm, or patriot mythology group.

Success in court depends on knowing and using the official rules to keep the judge and your opponent in line!

Previous Look-Up Tool Removed

Because government websites keep moving the rules around unexpectedly, it is impossible to keep updating the old look-up tool that once was here.

You are much more secure using Google®, rather than trusting that some "old" link presents the "latest and most up-to-date version" of the official rules.

Remember: Only the Rules rule!
